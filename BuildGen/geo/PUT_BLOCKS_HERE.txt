This folder is where the building blocks go.

It is empty on purpose. The blocks are Epic Games content from the free
"City Sample Buildings" pack on Fab and cannot be redistributed, so you
export them from your own copy of the pack - see README.txt, section
"GET THE BLOCKS".

The 10 meshes:

   SM_BLDG_SFE_L1_A1_Wall_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L1_A1_Wall_02_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L1_A1_CornerInL_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L1_A1_CornerInR_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L2_A1_Wall_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L2_A1_CornerInL_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L2_A1_CornerInR_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L3_A1_Wall_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L3_A1_CornerInL_01_N1
   SM_BLDG_SFE_L3_A1_CornerInR_01_N1

Short version: select the 10 meshes in Unreal, disable Nanite on them,
right-click -> "Send to Houdini", then press "Save Blocks From UE" on the
BuildGen node. The .bgeo.sc files land right here.

This is the default Blocks Folder ($HIP/geo). Keep the folder - the button
writes into it.
